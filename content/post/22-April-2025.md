The Singapore High Court recently ruled on the equal severity of all three types of rape under Section 375 of the Penal Code, dismissing an appeal from a convicted offender. The court emphasized that the legislative amendments in 2020 intended to treat all types of rape equally, regardless of the specific act committed.
